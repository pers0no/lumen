FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a date?

Send a request on our Contact page or give us a call. Your date is reserved once we receive a 30% non-refundable retainer.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Reschedule for free with at least 48 hours' notice. With later notice, your retainer can still be applied to a new date within 3 months.

What should I bring?

Two or three outfits without large logos, comfortable shoes, and a good mood. We'll email you a full prep guide a week before your session.

Do you provide RAW files?

You'll receive every color-corrected image in your online gallery. RAW files aren't included, since editing is part of our signature style.

How can I pay?

All major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Zelle, or cash at the studio. We invoice businesses with Net 15 terms.

Do I own my photos?

You get a personal-use print release for all delivered images. Businesses receive a commercial license for product and branding photos.

Don't see what you need?

Tell us about your project and we'll put together a custom quote.
