About us

A place where it's easy to be yourself

We opened Lumen in 2018 to photograph people honestly and beautifully, with no assembly line and no stiff, cookie-cutter poses.

The space

860 sq ft of natural light

High ceilings, floor-to-ceiling east- and south-facing windows, a white cyclorama wall, and several lifestyle sets: a bedroom, a loft kitchen, and a Scandinavian-style living room.

  • Private dressing room with a lighted vanity
  • Comfortable lounge with complimentary coffee and tea
  • Changing table and a kids' play corner
  • Free parking and an ADA-accessible elevator
Equipment

Everything's included

No need to haul gear. Lighting, modifiers, and backdrops are ready and waiting in the studio.

  • 4 Profoto strobes and 2 LED panels
  • Octaboxes, strip boxes, a beauty dish, and reflectors
  • 12 seamless paper backdrops in different colors
  • Fog machine and props for kids' sessions
